- [D2] "Why this is safe:": The safety argument omits the reachable second ACP turn after the proposed fresh bridge mount. Section 5e.3 records that kiro keeps the MCP client for the first child URL even after a later session/new, so closing that listener strands subsequent bridged calls. Fix: retain the child-scoped bridge, or demonstrate and test a kiro mechanism that replaces its cached client before relying on a new URL.
- [D3] "Then remove the resume branch of `resolveSession()`, so every turn takes the divergence branch: it retires the session, mounts a fresh bridge through `ensureChildBridge()`, and advertises the new URL to kiro in that turn's `session/new`.": This overturns the cited section 5e.3 decision that one child must retain one bridge URL because kiro cannot learn a replacement, while the Goal and Scope say that invariant and lifetime model remain unchanged. Fix: make the unref-only solution preserve the existing child-scoped bridge, or explicitly propose to supersede section 5e.3 with evidence that a new session/new replaces kiro's MCP client and regression coverage for multiple bridged turns.
- [D5] "- Target release: pi-kiro-agent 0.4.1 (patch)": The proposed removal of ACP session resumption and per-turn bridge replacement changes runtime behaviour users can observe, so docs/releasing.md classifies it as a minor rather than a patch; the spec also gives no release-policy reason for the chosen bump. Fix: either preserve existing session semantics and justify a patch, or target 0.5.0 and describe the user-visible behaviour change in the CHANGELOG.
